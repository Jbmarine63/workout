Privacy Policy

Workout (Android app, package com.jeremydoan.workout, version 1.5) and the Workout web app for iPhone (jbmarine63.github.io/workout)
Developer: Jeremy Doan · Effective date: November 17, 2026

In short: Workout works fully offline. Everything you enter, and anything you choose to read from Health Connect, stays on your phone. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send your information to the developer or to anyone else.

1. Information the app stores

To work, the app saves the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Workout logs: sessions, exercises, sets, reps, weights, times and notes
  • Your 16-week program progress (current week, finished days) and any training plans you import
  • Cardio entries: activity, minutes, optional distance and notes
  • Recovery: the injury areas you tick, recovery and stretching sessions you log, and the pain level (0–10) and notes you enter
  • Trackers you enter: body weight, body measurements, daily habits and water
  • Progress photos you choose to add (see section 3)
  • App settings (for example theme, Road mode, spoken cues, keep screen on)

The exercise library, demo animations, journal photos and articles are bundled inside the app. The developer cannot see, access or receive any of this information. Recovery content is general information, not medical advice.

2. Health Connect (Android, optional)

If you turn on Import from Health Connect in Trackers and grant permission, the app reads the following from Android Health Connect for the last 30 days: weight, steps, heart rate, sleep sessions and exercise sessions. Apps such as Samsung Health may write this data into Health Connect from your watch or band.

  • The app only reads. It never writes, changes or deletes anything in Health Connect.
  • The imported values are saved in the app's private storage on your phone so they can be shown in Trackers and Stats. They are never sent off the device, sold, shared, or used for advertising, credit or insurance decisions.
  • The use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You can turn the import off, remove all imported values in the app, and revoke access at any time in the Health Connect settings on your phone.

The iPhone web app does not connect to Apple Health or any health service; trackers there are entered by hand.

3. Photos and files

The app does not ask for access to your photo library or files. When you add a progress photo, you pick one image with the system photo picker; the app copies a smaller version into its private storage. When you export a backup (JSON) or a log (CSV), or import a backup, you choose the file with the system file picker. Backups list progress photos without the images.

4. What the app does not do

  • No sign-in or user accounts.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash reporting, tracking or profiling.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No network access: the Android app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send or receive data over the internet. The web app is downloaded once from GitHub Pages and then runs offline; it sends nothing back.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • Your information is never sold or shared.

Android permissions: Vibrate (rest timer buzz) and, only if you grant them, the Health Connect read permissions for weight, steps, heart rate, sleep and exercise.

5. Export, import and backups

The app only creates or reads backup files when you ask it to. Before an import replaces your data, the app keeps a safety copy on your phone. Android's own device backup (for example Google backup, if you turned it on) may include the app's data as part of your device backup; that backup is controlled by you and your backup provider under their privacy terms. The developer has no access to it.

6. Spoken cues (text-to-speech)

Spoken workout cues use the text-to-speech engine already on your phone. The app sends only the short cue text (for example “3, 2, 1, go” or an exercise name) to that engine on your device. It does not record audio or use the microphone.

7. Keeping or deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete individual entries in the app and remove imported Health Connect values from Trackers. You can erase everything by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, by clearing website data for the web app in iPhone Settings, or by uninstalling. The developer holds no copy, so it cannot be recovered afterwards.

8. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which your phone protects from other apps. Keep your phone protected with a screen lock to protect it further.

9. Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. No information leaves the device, so none is collected from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same location with a new effective date. Changes that collect any data would be explained clearly before they take effect.
